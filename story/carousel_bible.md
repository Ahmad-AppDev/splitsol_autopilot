# SplitSol carousels — rules for the writer

Format: a listicle carousel like "8 Things That Ruin Every Group Trip".
Audience: young Pakistanis (18–35) who share money with friends, flatmates, colleagues, partners.
Goal: relatable + useful so people SAVE and SHARE it, then download SplitSol.

## Topic pool (rotate, mix, invent new angles — never repeat a past title)
- group trips (Hunza, Murree, Skardu, Lahore food trips)
- flatmates / hostel / shared apartments (rent, bills, groceries, WiFi)
- office lunches, chai runs, team dinners, farewell gifts
- weddings & mehndi contributions, birthday gifts, surprise parties
- couples splitting dates and trips
- friend groups: the one who never pays back, the "I'll send later" friend
- money etiquette: how to ask for money back without being awkward
- signs you're the "bank" of your friend group

## Title patterns that work
- "8 Things That Ruin Every Group Trip"
- "7 Signs You're the Friend Who Always Pays"
- "6 Money Rules Every Flatmate Should Follow"
- "8 Ways to Ask for Your Money Back (Without Being Awkward)"
- "The 7 Types of Friends When the Bill Arrives"

## Rules
- 6 to 8 points. Headline ≤ 8 words. Body ≤ 22 words, one or two short sentences.
- Funny, specific, Pakistani context (rupees as "Rs 4,200", chai, dhaba, Careem, JazzCash, Easypaisa).
- At most ONE point may mention SplitSol, and only naturally (e.g. "use an app that splits it to the paisa").
- Never use the phrase "who owes who" or "who owes whom". Say it another way.
- No real people, no real brands except SplitSol, JazzCash, Easypaisa, Careem.
- Image descriptions: a simple illustrated scene, no text or numbers in the image.
- Caption: one hook line, one line asking to save/share ("Send this to the friend who still owes you 👀"),
  then "SplitSol — split bills fairly. Link in bio." and 4–6 hashtags.
