# THE TRIP — SplitSol story series bible

## Premise
You (the viewer, "you", second person) planned a 5-day road trip to Hunza with four friends.
You paid the hotel deposit upfront. Nobody tracks anything. Every day the money gets messier,
and the friendship meter drops. The audience decides what you do by commenting one of two words.
The NEXT episode always follows the winning comment.

## Format (every episode)
- 20–40 seconds, 4–6 scenes, calm narrator voice in second person ("You're on day 3...").
- Scene 1 opens with the hook line: "You're on a trip with 4 friends, and you paid for everything."
  (vary wording slightly each day, keep the idea).
- Middle: today's money dilemma, escalating, specific, relatable for Pakistani friend groups.
- Last scene: the cliffhanger + the choice. Narration ends with "Comment X or Y."
- Tone: dry, funny, slightly dramatic. Never preachy. Short punchy sentences.

## Cast (keep names and traits consistent)
- YOU — the main character. Always drawn the same (see character sheet). Never speaks on screen.
- Bilal — always "forgets" his wallet. Charming. Owes the most.
- Hamza — says "I'll send it later, yaar." Never does.
- Sana — the organiser. Has a notes-app list nobody reads.
- Faraz — rich kid. Rounds everything up, then argues about Rs 50.

## Stats on screen (update every episode, carry over in state.json)
- PAID: total rupees YOU have paid so far (only goes up).
- YOUR SHARE: what your fair share actually is (lower than PAID).
- FRIENDSHIP: percent, starts 100%, drops with each messy money moment, can rise slightly after good choices.

## SplitSol placement
- Days 1–4: no product. Pure story. Build the mess.
- Day 5 or 6: at peak chaos, Sana (or YOU, if the audience chose it) opens an app on her phone:
  SplitSol. One tap and everything is settled. FRIENDSHIP jumps.
- After that, SplitSol shows up naturally (a phone screen in a scene, a mention), never a hard sell.
- Caption always ends with: "SplitSol — link in bio".

## Hard rules
- Never use the phrase "who owes who" or "who owes whom". Say it another way
  ("who paid what", "what everyone's share is", "settling up").
- No real people, no real brands other than SplitSol.
- Money in Pakistani rupees, written "Rs 4,200".
- Keep each narration line under 25 words.
- Vote options are ONE word each, ALL CAPS, easy to type (e.g. COVER / CALLOUT).

## Season arc (guide, not a script — follow the votes)
1. Departure. You paid the hotel deposit. Bilal "forgot" his wallet at the petrol pump. COVER or CALLOUT.
2. Roadside dinner in Chilas. Split equal or pay for what you ordered? EQUAL or EXACT.
3. Hamza's "I'll send it later" becomes a running joke. CHASE or WAIT.
4. Faraz rounds up the jeep hire, then fights over Rs 50 chai. Group chat explodes. MUTE or REPLY.
5. Peak chaos at Attabad Lake: nobody knows what anyone paid. Sana opens SplitSol. TRY or IGNORE.
6. One-tap settle up. Bilal finally pays. FRIENDSHIP recovers. Next trip? YES or NEVER.
7+. Keep going: new trips, new friend-group money situations, same characters.
